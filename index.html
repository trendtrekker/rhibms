About Our Institution

Excellence in Biomedical Training, Technology & Leadership

The Redemption Higher Institute of Biomedical and Management Sciences (RHIBMS) is a premier tertiary institution headquartered in Buea, Southwest Region, Cameroon. Established in 2010 under the authorization of the Ministry of Public Health and the Ministry of Higher Education, RHIBMS has evolved into a powerhouse for healthcare practitioners, engineers, and business leaders.

Our mission is simple yet transformative: to deliver world-class, practice-oriented education that empowers young Africans to solve urgent public health emergencies, pioneer technological innovation, and drive sustainable economic development.

Why Choose RHIBMS

A Legacy of High-Impact Healthcare & Vocational Training

We combine rigorous clinical science with modern equipment and compassionate care. Here is why thousands of families trust RHIBMS to build their children’s future.

Accredited State Nursing

Approved by Cameroon’s Ministry of Public Health. Our State Registered Nursing (SRN) graduates enjoy immediate professional validation and high hospital employability.

Modern Diagnostic Labs

From clinical biochemistry and hematology to pharmacy dispensing, students gain real hands-on experience using industry standard equipment and clinical simulations.

Expert Clinical Lecturers

Our faculty brings together practicing medical doctors, senior head nurses, laboratory scientists, civil engineers, and experienced business consultants.

Startup Capital Awards

We don't just train students to seek jobs; we fund future employers! Graduating classes receive competitive seed capital grants to establish healthcare clinics and tech startups.

Affordable Tuition (250,000 FCFA)

High quality shouldn't mean out of reach. All our HND programs are maintained at an accessible standard fee with flexible installment options for families.

International Degree Pathways

Direct linkages with our US partner (University of Maryland Eastern Shore) allow ambitious students to complete degree upgrades and pursue international certifications.
