Our Story & Purpose

Built on Integrity, Practical Competence, and Social Impact

The Redemption Higher Institute of Biomedical and Management Sciences (RHIBMS) was founded in 2010 to answer a profound societal challenge: the shortage of practically trained, compassionate, and ethically grounded healthcare professionals and technical entrepreneurs in Cameroon.

Recognized and licensed under the Ministry of Public Health (MINSANTE) and the Ministry of Higher Education (MINESUP), RHIBMS has evolved from a specialized healthcare academy into a multi-disciplinary higher institute encompassing health sciences, engineering technology, agribusiness, and management studies.

Our Mission

To deliver accessible, high-caliber vocational and biomedical training through state-of-the-art simulation labs, expert clinical mentorship, and partnerships that produce certified, employable graduates.

Our Vision

To stand as Central Africa’s premier center of vocational medicine, technological innovation, and ethical leadership, producing problem-solvers who transform health and enterprise nationwide.
