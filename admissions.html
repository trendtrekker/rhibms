Eligibility Criteria

Admission Requirements by Faculty

School of Health Sciences (Nursing, Lab, Pharmacy)

Minimum of 2 GCE A-Level passes in science subjects (Biology, Chemistry, Physics, or Mathematics), or Baccalauréat Series C or D. Medical fitness certificate and an orientation interview.

School of Engineering & Technology (Computer Science, Civil)

Passes in at least 2 GCE A-Levels in scientific/technical subjects, or Baccalauréat Series C, D, TI, E, or F4. Pass in Mathematics or Physics at Ordinary Level or equivalent is required.

School of Management Sciences & Agriculture

At least 2 GCE A-Level passes in any discipline (Arts, Commercial, or Sciences), or Baccalauréat Series A, ABI, or G. Minimum 4 GCE O-Level passes including English and Mathematics.

Required Application Documents:

  • Certified copies of GCE O/L & A/L results slips or Baccalauréat certificate
  • Certified copy of Birth Certificate
  • 4 recent passport-size color photographs (white background)
  • Medical certificate of fitness from an authorized government hospital
